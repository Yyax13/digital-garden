# Huainanzi

The Huainanzi is an ancient philosophical and governmental Chinese text made up of essays from scholarly debates held at the court of Lui An, before 139 BCE. Compiled as a compendium for an enlightened sovereign and his court, the work attempts to define the conditions for a perfect sociopolitical order, derived mainly from a perfect ruler.
