# Fengsu Tongyi

Fengsu Tongyi (Chinese: 風俗通義; lit. 'Comprehensive Meaning of Customs and Mores'), also known as Fengsu Tong (Chinese: 风俗通), is a book written about 195 CE by Ying Shao.
