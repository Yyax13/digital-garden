# The Great Flood of Gun-Yu

The Great Flood of Gun-Yu, also known as the Gun-Yu myth, was a major flood in the ancient China.

![[Others/History/Empires, Kingdoms and Dynasties/China/Assets/The Great Flood Map.png]]

## The origin of the Flood

In the reign of [[Emperor Yao]] that the Great Flood began, a flood so vast that no part of Yao's territory was spared.
