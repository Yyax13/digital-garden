# Suiren

Suiren (Chinese:  燧人; lit. "Flint Person"), also known as Suihuang (Chinese: 燧皇; lit. "Flint Emperor"), appears in Chinese mythology and some works which draw upon it.

He is mentioned in ten books from the Han Dynasty or before. Those crediting him with the introduction of drilling wood for fire include three Confucian works.

He is also considered one of the legendary [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors]] by the Baihu Tongyi and Shangshu dazhuan.
