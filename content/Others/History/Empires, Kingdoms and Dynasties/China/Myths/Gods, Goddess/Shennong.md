# Shennong

Shennong (Chinese: 神農), variously translated as "Divine Farmer" or "Divine Husbandman", born Jiang Shinian (姜石年), was a mythological Chinese ruler known as the first Yan Emperor who has become a deity in Chinese folk religion. He is venerated as a culture hero in China.

Shennong has at times been counted amongst the [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors|Three Sovereigns]].
