# Earthly Sovereign

The Earthly Sovereign (Chinese: 地皇) was the second legendary Chinese king after [[Pangu]]'s era.
According to [[Yiwen Leiju]], the Earthly Sovereign came after [[Heavenly Sovereign]], the first of the [[Three Sovereigns and Five Emperors|Three Sovereigns]].
