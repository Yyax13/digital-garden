# Sima Qian

Sima Qian (c. 145 BCE to 86 BCE) was a Chinese historian during the early Han Dynasty. He is considered the father of Chinese historiography for the [[Shiji]], a general history of China covering more than two thousand years from the rise of [[Yellow Emperor]].
