A _thearch_ is the ruler of a _thearchy_, a system of divine government or a hierarchy of deities. The term comes from Greek, combining _theos_ (god) and _arche_ (ruler). It can refer to a god who acts as a supreme ruler, or a mortal monarch believed to rule as a god.
