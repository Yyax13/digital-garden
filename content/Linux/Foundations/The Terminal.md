## Introduction

A terminal (or a Terminal Emulator, as the original term was used for the machine used when the development of complex computers and operational systems began) is the software that an user uses to reach the shell. It works like the front-end for the shell, letting users easily write commands for being interpreted by the shell.

Some examples of terminal emulators are:

1. [GNOME Terminal](https://github.com/GNOME/gnome-terminal)
2. [Kitty](https://github.com/kovidgoyal/kitty)
3. [Ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty)
4. [Tmux](https://github.com/tmux/tmux)
